## About the Series
***The Walking Dead*** is a **post-apocalyptic horror drama** that aired on *AMC* from **2010 to 2022**. It is based on the comic book series by Robert Kirkman, Tony Moore, and Charlie Adlard. The story follows a group of survivors trying to stay alive in a world overrun by walkers, and shows that the living can be as dangerous as the dead.

> "We are all infected." - Rick Grimes

---

## Main Characters
1. **Rick Grimes** (Andrew Lincoln): a sheriff's deputy who becomes the group's leader
2. **Daryl Dixon** (Norman Reedus): a skilled tracker and loyal fighter
3. **Michonne** (Danai Gurira): a sword-wielding survivor
4. **Negan** (Jeffrey Dean Morgan): the group's most feared enemy, who later changes sides

## Why I Like It
- Intense, suspenseful storytelling
- Characters who grow and change over the seasons
- Hard moral choices:
  - Survival vs. humanity
  - Trust vs. betrayal
- ~~Boring episodes~~ Almost none!
